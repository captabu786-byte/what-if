        Frequently asked questions

        What is WHAT IF?

        WHAT IF? is an interactive decision and scenario simulator for exploring possible outcomes before making a real-world decision.

        Can I use WHAT IF? for business ideas?

        Yes. Try questions about starting a business, e-commerce, budgets, pricing, growth or side hustles. Results are planning estimates, not guarantees.

        Can I use it for investing?

        You can structure an investing scenario and explore assumptions. It is not financial advice and does not guarantee returns.

        Can I use it for moving abroad?

        Yes. Explore broad costs, risks and planning steps. Verify immigration and legal requirements with official authorities.

        Are the results guaranteed?

        No. Results are illustrative estimates based on simplified assumptions and cannot predict real-world outcomes.

        WHAT IF? is a decision-support experiment. Estimates are illustrative and are not financial, legal, immigration or business advice.
